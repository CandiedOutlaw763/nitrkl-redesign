Biography

Dr. Sibarama Panigrahi is currently working as an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NIT Rourkela, Odisha, India. He has completed his B.Tech, M.Tech. and Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering. He has been awarded research grants from major funding agencies in India, including the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) and the Odisha State Higher Education Council (OSHEC). As part of an OSHEC-sponsored project, he has developed and deployed an electricity load forecasting software for Tata Power Western Odisha Distribution Limited (TPWODL), one of India’s leading power distribution companies. He has over 10 years of experience in teaching and research. He has authored and co-authored over fifty research papers published in reputed international journals and conferences. His research interests include Soft Computing, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, and Time Series Forecasting. He has served as a reviewer of more than 30 reputed international SCI Journals. He has also served as a project reviewer for ANRF and BRNS (Department of Atomic Energy) projects. He is an Associate Editor of the PLOS ONE Journal and the Scientific Reports Journal.

Laboratory Development
  • Intelligent Data Analytics Laboratory, National Institute of Technology Rourkela, The Intelligent Data Analytics Laboratory (IDAL) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, National Institute of Technology Rourkela is a research and innovation lab dedicated to advancing intelligent computing, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data-driven decision-making technologies. The laboratory focuses on developing efficient analytical models and intelligent systems for solving real-world problems in domains such as healthcare, smart agriculture, energy systems, forecasting, and industrial automation. IDAL promotes interdisciplinary research by integrating techniques from data analytics, deep learning, optimization, signal processing, and cloud/edge computing. The laboratory supports undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral research activities through state-of-the-art computational facilities, collaborative projects, and industry-oriented applications. It also actively organizes workshops, internships, and skill development programs to foster innovation and practical learning in emerging areas of intelligent data science and analytics.
