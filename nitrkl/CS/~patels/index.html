Biography

Sanjeev Patel received the B.Tech. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad, India, in 2005 the M. Tech. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi, India in 2008 and the Ph.D. degree from the School of Computer and Systems Sciences, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi, India in 2017. He is currently working as an Assistant Professor Grade-I in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Rourkela, since 20th March, 2020. Prior to joining this institute, he had worked at Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, Noida, UP, India from 2011 to 18th March, 2020 and also worked at Jaypee University of Information Technology, Waknaghat, Solan, HP, India from 2008 to 2010. He guided many graduate projects as well as a few postgraduate dissertations. He is guiding 3 PhD students out of which 2 have submitted the thesis. The current areas of research are cloud computing, performance modelling of flow and congestion control algorithms, stochastic processes, optimization techniques, machine learning, multimedia communication and image processing.
