Biography

Prof. K.K.Khatua, Professor in the Civil Engineering Department of NIT Rourkela, has more than 28 years of teaching, and research experience in the fields of River Hydraulics, Computational hydro dynamics, Computational hydrology and Fluid Mechanics etc. Apart from many administrative, academic and research activities, he was the Dean( AIIR) of NITR from July 2021 to 2023. He is presently working as the Coordinater and Head of cMahanadi Basin Management and studies at NIT Rourkela Prof. Khatua is a Fellow of The Institution of Engineers (India) and a fellow of the Indian Society for Hydraulics. He has published over 100 reputed International Journals and more than 200 peer-reviewed international conference papers. He has Research collaborations with INRAE, Lyon France University of South Carolina, USA. Lough-borough University, UK. University of Birmingham, UK, University of Leeds, UK, Kyoto University, Japan. He has also research collaborations with reputed IITs, NITs and Research Institutes. He has guided 15 Ph. D. and more than 120 M. Tech thesis. Several Best M. Tech and PhD theses have been awarded through his guidance in different years at the Institute and national levels. He has completed many high-value research projects as PI through DST core research grants, UGC-UKIERI and IEI research grants and has contributed to international research projects with INRAE, France, and with EPSRC grant of UK. He also contributed to the Groundwater survey for All India Radio, Bridge protection projects of Indian Railways, and flood management issues Water Resources Government of Odisha, Rourkela, Prof. Khatua is an editorial board member of many reputed International journals. He is also an expert member/executive member of high-level committees in government departments and at different universities and organizations. He has achieved many national and International recognitions, medals and awards, such as the “Best Teacher Award 2021” at NIT Rourkela.
