A : General Academic Administration

NIT Rourkela is affiliated to which University/Body?

NIT Rourkela is an Institute of National Importance and is an Autonomous Body under the Ministry of Education, GOI.

Whether NIT Rourkela is a registered Institute?

Yes, NIT Rourkela is registered under Societies Act and its Registration No is 21406/210.

What are the different programmers offered by NIT Rourkela?

NIT Rourkela offers UG, PG and Research programs. Under UG programmer there are 14 B.Tech (4Years), 14 Dual Degree B.Tech & M.Tech (5years), B.Arch (5years), 4 Integrated M.Sc. (5years) programmers are offered. Under PG programmers, there are 28 M.Tech (2years), 5 M.Sc. (2years), MA in Development Studies (2years) and MBA (2years) programmers are offered. Under Research programmers, PhD, M.Tech (By Research), PDF, RA and D. Sc. Programmes are offered. For details please refer respective Academic Regulations in the link http://website.nitrkl.ac.in/Academics/AcademicProcess/Academic-Regulations/

Where one can find Academic Regulations governing different programmers at NIT Rourkela?

Academic regulations governing different programmers offered by NIT Rourkela are available in the website link http://website.nitrkl.ac.in/Academics/AcademicProcess/Academic-Regulations/

What is the fee Structure for different programmers offered by NIT Rourkela?

Fee Structure for different programmers offered by NIT Rourkela is given in the Appendix-IV of the respective Academic Regulations given in the link mentioned in A4 above.

What is the FAX number of the Institute?

0661-2462033 (Academic matters), 0661-2462022 (Administrative matters)

Where can I find the Academic Calendar of the Institute?

Academic Calendar of the Institute is available in the website link http://website.nitrkl.ac.in/Academics/AcademicProcess/Academic-calendar/

B : Admission

How can one take admission in Undergraduate programme (B.Tech, B.Arch, Dual Degree B.Tech & M.Tech, Integrated M.Sc.) at NIT Rourkela?

One can take admission to the Undergraduate programme (B.Tech, B.Arch, Dual Degree B.Tech & M.Tech, Integrated M.Sc.) at NIT Rourkela only through Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB) after qualifying JEE (Main) examination of the same year. No other means of admission is available at NIT Rourkela.

How can one take admission in M.Tech programme at NIT Rourkela?

One can take admission to M.Tech programme through Common Counselling for M.Tech(CCMT) after qualifying GATE. If seats remain vacant after GATE qualified candidates, in some specific low popular Specializations, admission can be given to Non-GATE candidates.

What are the other Postgraduate programmes offered by NIT Rourkela?

Apart from M.Tech, NIT Rourkela offers Postgraduate programmes in M.Sc. ( in Applied Geology, Chemistry, Life Science, Mathematics and physics), MBA and MA in Development Studies. All these are 2 years (4 semesters) programmes.

Whether NIT Rourkela offers MCA programme?

No. NIT Rourkela has discontinued MCA programme since 2007.

How can one take admission in 2 years M.Sc. programme at NIT Rourkela?

One can take admission to 2 years M.Sc. programme at NIT Rourkela through IIT JAM conducted by one of the IITs.

How can one take admission in MBA programme at NIT Rourkela?

Admission to MBA programme at NIT Rourkela is done through an open advertisement and Institutional selection process. Advertisement comes during the month of January of the previous Academic Year which contains all details including selection process.

Whether selection for admission to MBA programme is done through CAT score or any other score?

Selection for admission to MBA programme is not done purely through CAT score or any other score of the examination meant for management studies. However, these scores are given due weightage in the selection process along with Career Mark, Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

How can one take admission in MA in Development Studies programme at NIT Rourkela?

One can apply to NIT Rourkela against the open advertisement made by NIT Rourkela during the month of April May of the previous Academic Year. Details regarding eligibility and selection are mentioned in the advertisement.

After taking admission to a programme paying full fee if a student cancels his/her admission, how much money he/she will get back?

For the programmes for which admission is done through central bodies like CSAB (for UG), CCMT( for M.Tech), JAM (for 2 year M.Sc.) etc. , if a student cancels/ surrenders his/her admission before the last date for seat surrender for the last round of admission so that his/her seat can be included in the vacancy list, he/she will get back full money after deducting Rs. 1000/- from the admission fee and the balance of Mess advance after adjustment of dues, if any.

F : Scholarship

Whether any scholarship is given to SC/ST students at NIT Rourkela?

SC/ST students are eligible for Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) of the respective State Governments if they satisfy the norms set by the State Govt. For that they need to apply in appropriate time to their State Govts.

Among the admitted students in the first year top 12 SC students and top 5 ST students are eligible to get TOP CLASS SCHOLARSHIP from the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, GOI and the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, GOI respectively provided their parental annual income is below 4.5 lakhs. These students will get Top Class scholarship for four years (first year to fourth year) provided they get promoted to the next higher year, else the scholarship will be stopped.

A student who will get Top Class Scholarship will not be eligible for Post Matric Scholarship. However, he/she may get PMS if his Top Class scholarship is stopped.

How many scholarships a student can avail at a time?

Only one. A student getting scholarship from one Govt. fund is not eligible for the 2nd scholarship.

Whether all students taken admission to M.Tech programme with valid GATE score will get GATE scholarship?

Yes, they will get GATE Scholarship of Rs.8000/- per month paid every month subject to satisfying attendance requirement of the Institute.

Is there any scholarship available for Non-GATE students taken admission to M.Tech programme?

Non-GATE students taken admission to M.Tech programme and belonging to SC/ST categories are eligible for Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) of the respective State Governments if they satisfy the norms set by the State Govt. For that they need to apply in appropriate time to their State Govts.

There is no provision of scholarship to the Non-GATE students of other categories. However, some students satisfying eligibility norms, may be given financial assistance from TEQIP – II fund till available.

Whether an SC/ST M.Tech student getting GATE scholarship can avail PMS scholarship?

No, an SC/ST M.Tech student getting GATE scholarship can not avail any other scholarship.

Under which condition a PhD student will get scholarship/fellowship?

A PhD student having M.Tech/M.Pharm degree and within the age limit are eligible for Institute research fellowship for four years; first two years @ Rs.31000/- a month and next two years on satisfactory performance @ Rs.35000/- a month.

The maximum age limit is 32 years (35 years for SC/ST/Women candidates) to be eligible for fellowship.

Apart from Institute research fellowship, PhD students are eligible for External fellowships like Rajib Gandhi National Fellowship, Inspire Fellowship, Maulana Azad Fellowship, CSIR Fellowship etc. if they qualify for the said fellowship examinations and satisfy their terms and conditions.

Is there any scholarship for M.Tech(Research) students?

M.Tech(Research) students are eligible for Institute research fellowship provided they have valid GATE/NET/GPAT score and are within the age limit of 28 years (31 years for SC/ST/Women candidates). Such students will get fellowship maximum for two years @ Rs.12400/- a month.

Whether a Sponsored student satisfying fellowship norms and within the age limit will get any fellowship?

No, Sponsored students are not eligible for any type of fellowship.

Whether a student needs to return the scholarship/fellowship if he/she discontinues the programme?

M.Tech, M.Tech(Res) and PhD students need to return the scholarship/fellowship amount received till date to the Institute if he/she discontinues the programme. This is not applicable to the students of other programmes.

G : Foreign Students

What are the different schemes under which a foreign student can take admission?

At NIT Rourkela a foreign student can take admission under DASA, ICCR and MEA schemes.

Can a foreign student take admission in any programme offered by NIT Rourkela?

Yes, a foreign student can take admission in any programme offered by NIT Rourkela provided he/she satisfies academic eligibility of NIT Rourkela.

What is the fee structure for a foreign student taking admission at NIT Rourkela?

Tuition fee for different schemes are as follows:

  1. DASA: US $ 7000 (US $ 3500 for students from SAARC countries)
  2. ICCR: US $ 4000 (US $ 2000 for students from SAARC countries)
  3. MEA (Welfare): As applicable to Indian students All other fees are same as applicable to Indian students (See A4 & A5) above.
Is there separate hostel for foreign students?

No, there is no separate hostel for foreign students at NIT Rourkela.
