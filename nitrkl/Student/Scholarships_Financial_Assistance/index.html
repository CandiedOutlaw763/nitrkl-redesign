Scholarships for students are designed to support meritorious and deserving candidates in pursuing quality technical education without financial constraints. At NIT Rourkela, meritorious students are encouraged through a wide range of scholarship schemes aimed at recognizing academic excellence, fostering innovation, and promoting inclusive access to education. The Institute also provides financial assistance in the form of selective scholarships and fee waivers to students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. Special scholarship schemes are available for SC, ST, OBC, Economically Weaker Section (EWS), and female students. These scholarships may cover tuition fees, hostel charges, or provide monthly stipends, enabling students to focus on academic learning, research, and skill development. Scholarships at NIT Rourkela are offered through multiple sources, including alumni contributions, corporate sponsors, State Governments, and the Government of India. A well-defined procedure is followed for availing scholarships, and each application is carefully scrutinized and verified before approval.
