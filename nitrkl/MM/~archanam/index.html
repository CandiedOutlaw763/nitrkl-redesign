Biography

Prof. Archana Mallik is a faculty member in the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Rourkela, India. She earned her B.E. in Metallurgical Engineering from Indira Gandhi Institute of Technology, Sarang, her M.Tech. in Metallurgical Engineering from Banaras Hindu University (BHU), and her Ph.D. from NIT Rourkela. She was also awarded the prestigious Fulbright-Nehru Postdoctoral Fellowship, during which she conducted research at the University of California, Berkeley. Prof. Mallik's research focuses on electrochemical materials processing, corrosion science, thin films and coatings, graphene synthesis, nanocomposites, and advanced surface engineering. She has successfully led several sponsored research projects funded by agencies such as the Department of Science and Technology (DST), the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), and the Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB). She has published extensively in leading international journals and has made significant contributions to the development of advanced materials and sustainable coating technologies. With a distinguished career in teaching, research, and academic leadership, Prof. Mallik continues to mentor undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral students while contributing to advancements in metallurgical and materials engineering through high-impact research and national and international collaborations.
