Biography

Prof. Anindya Basu is currently serving as Professor in the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Rourkela, India. He obtained his B.E. in Metallurgical Engineering from Bengal Engineering College (now IIEST Shibpur), followed by an M.Tech and Ph.D. in Metallurgical and Materials Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. Prof. Basu possesses over seventeen years of academic experience and has previously spent five years in the automobile industry, specializing in heat treatment processes. His research interests primarily encompass Physical Metallurgy and Surface Engineering, with a focus on surface modification techniques, corrosion behavior, metal-joining and phase transformation. He has guided several Ph.D. and postgraduate students and has published extensively in reputed international journals. Prof. Basu has also led multiple sponsored research projects funded by national agencies such as DST, CSIR, and SERB. In addition to his research and teaching, he actively contributes to institutional administration.
