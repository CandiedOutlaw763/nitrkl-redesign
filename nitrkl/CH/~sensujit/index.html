Biography

Dr. Sujit Sen is a Professor in the Department of Chemical Engineering at NIT Rourkela. He earned his Ph.D. from the Department of Chemical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur in 2011. Following this, he joined BITS Pilani, Rajasthan, as a Lecturer and later transitioned to NIT Rourkela as an Assistant Professor in the same year. Prof. Sen has a versatile area of research interests, experience and expertise focusing on real industrial and environmental issues that address UN’s Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) – starting from cradle-to-cradle solutions to industrial waste to value-added material, wastewater treatment, environmental catalysis, green and sustainable production of fine and platform chemicals, carbon capture and conversion, etc. He has successfully secured numerous prestigious projects from organizations such as ANRF-DST, ISRO, CSIR, and MHRD. His contributions include numerous publications in high-impact journals and patents, along with the development of patented technologies for the low-cost production of zeolites. In recognition of his achievements, Prof. Sen received the “Fast-Track Young Scientist Award” from DST in 2013 for his research in hydrogen sulfide gas capture and fine chemical synthesis. He was also honored as part of the “2019 Class of Influential Researchers” by the American Chemical Society for his work on arsenic remediation. Furthermore, he secured a multi-institutional IMPRINT-2 project in 2019, where he developed a prototype to effectively treat real industrial wastewater. Prof. Sen is the founding faculty advisor for the American Institute of Chemical Engineers (AIChE) student chapter at NIT Rourkela. He actively participates in the institute's environmental sustainability initiatives and has authored the Annual Sustainability Report. In his role as Chairperson of the Centre of Intellectual Property Rights, he oversees awareness activities and strategic management of the Institute's IPR.
