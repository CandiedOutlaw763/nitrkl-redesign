  • Central Library

    Biju Patnaik Central Library (BPCL) of NIT Rourkela, named after the legendary industrialist and statesman, and the founding Chairman of Board of Governors of REC Rourkela, is functioning since 1965. It acts as main learning resource centre of the institute which constitutes robust collections in both print and electronic resources. The central library supports teaching and research activities of faculty, students and research scholars. For more details visit http://library.nitrkl.ac.in/. Phone : (0661) 246 2101

  • Halls of Residence

    Large, exclusively student-occupied accommodation, maintained by the university. Residents live in study bedrooms, either alone or sharing with another student. The University, with its beautifully landscaped and carefully tended gardens, provides an ideal environment for a congenial learning experience. Here is a campus that invites students out of their hostels to spend time between classes midst greenery and a riot of floral color. The Institute is fully residential and provides accommodation facilities for all students. The Institute have total 11 hostels which includes 7 boys hostel, 2 girls hostel and 2 married hostel (only for research scholars)

  • Sports and Fitness

    The Institute provides its students with world-class sports facilities which include a Football –Hockey stadium named DILIP TIRCKEY STADIUM. It also houses a gymnasium with all the modern facilities. The gymnasium also has 2 instructors to help the students. Apart from that, we also have the SACHIN TENDULKAR CRICKET GROUND equipped with flood lights. We also have two tennis courts, a basketball court with separate timings for male and female students.

    Every hall of residence boasts of a Badminton court and a Volleyball court for its boarders. It also provides facilities for Table Tennis, Carom and Chess. Some of the hostels are also equipped with a modern gym with the latest equipments.

  • Swimming Pool

    NIT also offers separate swimming facilities for men and women students. The Men's 50 m x 25 m floodlit swimming pool has eight lanes and the necessary facilities to conduct even international-level competitions. The floodlit women's swimming pool is 25m x 12m, and is in the new Women's Hostel premises. Separate swimming coaches for men and women are available at particular times at the pools, which are open from 6.00 hr to 20.30 hrs every day. Some special events like inter NIT, Inter Hall or any other swimming competition are also organised in the swimming pool.

  • Canteens

    The campus has a couple of canteens, the calorie canteen and SLR canteen located between the Audio Visual Hall and department of CSE. Another canteen, Hexagon, located beside the hostels, frequented more perhaps by hostelites looking for a change from hostel grub than the occasional visitor. The Campus Cafe is open from 8.00 hrs to 20.00 hrs on all working days including Saturdays. A wide variety of South Indian and North Indian dishes are available. During lunch time Meals are available at very nominal rates. Contacts: 0661-2462973

  • Health Centre

    The Institute Health centre has separate two-storeyed building which is located in the heart of the campus. Health Centre provides the health services to the Institute employees, their dependants, students and non-entitled patients on outpatient basis. The Health centre has fifteen numbers of staff including five numbers of full time allopathic doctors, three visiting specialist doctors and seven supporting staff. Drugs are disbursed to the beneficiaries free of cost on doctor’s prescription, generated online through M/s Apollo Pharmacy present just beside the Health Centre. Patients in need of advanced care are referred to five designated hospitals in Rourkela, where all indoor treatment is provided free of cost.

  • Banks & ATMs

    The institute has one bank situated in the campus, State Bank of India. The bank is regular branche in the campus and is housed in separate buildings near the residential area of faculty. Application forms for important national level exams such as JEE, GATE and CAT are sold in these banks. Institute employees have their salaries credited to the State Bank of India. State Bank of India has two ATMs - one located near the Main gate premises and the other near the hostel area. One AXIS bank ATM is hteir inside the Institute academic area. Contacts: 0661-2462975

  • Guest Houses

    The Institute has two guest houses within the campus. The guest houses are near the Directors banglow. The one in front of the banglow is South Block and the one behind the banglow is North Guest House. The South Block Guest House has altogether 18 A/c Suites with a telephone and a TV in each room. VIPs, Institute guests and invited guests are usually accommodated here. The North Block Guest House has 15 rooms in total all are Air-conditioned rooms, This provides boarding and lodging facilities for the Institute guests and Visitors, newly appointed faculty, staff members, delegates and participants attending various Conferences, Seminars, Symposia and Workshops. Both the guest houses are operated as a non-profit activity to mainly support the academic and research activity on the campus with a homely atmosphere and ambiance traditionally acclaimed for its environs of hygiene and food of homely relish and richness.

  • Security

    NIT Rourkela has developed an efficient security system. The Security Wing of the Institute has laid down a set of rules and regulations which have to be complied with by all residents and visitors on campus. The Security Wing is headed by the Chief Security Officer and has a Duty room functioning round-the-clock to prevent any breach of security. It is the responsibility of all the residents of the campus that they inform the Chief Security Officer / Duty Officer / Duty Room immediately on noticing any mishap, suspicious movements of persons, any untoward incident happening and/or, information regarding probability of any of these. Timely information is of vital importance. NIT Rourkela, falls under the jurisdiction of Sector 3 police Station.Any case warranting Police Cover / Assistance should be reported to the Inspector of Police of the respective Police Stations. Such incidents should also be brought to the notice of CSO for internal enquiry. It must be understood that the Security wing of the institute has no policing or constabulary powers. It has only watch and ward responsibility.

  • Kendriya Vidyalaya NIT Rourkela

    Kendriya Vidyalaya NIT Rourkela is established as a significant welfare initiative by the National Institute of Technology Rourkela with an objective of strengthening quality school education. The school commenced its journey in November 2023 under the Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) sector and is functioning under the administrative control of Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan, Ministry of Education, Government of India. At present, the Vidyalaya is operating from a temporary facility located within a section of the Technology Innovation, Incubation and Research (TIIR) Building on the NIT Rourkela campus. In 2026, the Vidyalaya is scheduled to shift to a state-of-the-art permanent school building within the NIT Rourkela premises, which will further enhance the learning environment and institutional facilities. The entire school infrastructure has been developed through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) funding provided by Coal India Limited, reflecting a strong commitment towards improving educational standards and ensuring inclusive access to education. In alignment with the national vision of Viksit Bharat@2047, which highlights the importance of quality education in nation-building, the Vidyalaya aims to contribute positively to society by serving students from diverse backgrounds and promoting social equity. Currently, Kendriya Vidyalaya NIT Rourkela is a one-section school offering classes from Class I to Class VI, with one section in each class; the highest class as of 2024 is Class V. The school is located in Sundargarh district of Odisha, falling under the Rourkela Assembly Constituency and Sundargarh Parliamentary Constituency.
