Biography

Monalisa Pattnaik (SMIEEE) received B.Tech. degree in Electrical Engineering from the College of Engineering and Technology, Orissa University of Agriculture and Technology, Bhubaneswar, India, in 1999. She received M.Tech. and Ph.D. degree from Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India, in 2006 and 2013 respectively. Dr. Pattnaik is a recipient of the POSOCO Power System Award, Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd., India, in 2013. Dr. Monalisa Pattnaik has been honored with the IEEE Kolkata Section Award for “Outstanding Volunteer – 2025”, recognizing her exceptional dedication, leadership, and service to the professional community. She is currently a Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering, National Institute of Technology Rourkela, India. Her prime areas of teaching and research include Wind and Solar Energy System, Hybrid Electric Vehicle, Efficient Power Management of Hybrid Microgrid, Grid Integration issues of RES, Machine Drives and Power Electronics. Dr. Pattnaik has successfully developed Grid Integrated Microgrid and Electric Vehicle and Renewable Energy System Laboratories at the Department of Electrical Engineering, NIT Rourkela, India. She has supervised 05 PhD students and published 25 research papers in reputed journals including IEEE Transactions, IET, Elsevier, and Wiley journals, 5 book chapters, more than 55 conference papers and 02 granted patents. She has organised 02 GIAN courses, 06 STC & FDPs, 05 Workshops and 01 IEEE conference. She is also involved in research project work sponsored by funding agencies such as DST (Indo-Sri Lanka Joint Research Program), ANRF (CRG, FIST, PAIR Schemes), DRDO, NITR-DF Board, I-Hub, IIIT Hyderabad etc. She is the founding Chair of IEEE IA & PE Joint Societies- Rourkela and founding Advisor of IEEE IAS, IES & PES student branch chapters NIT Rourkela. She has been nominated and participated in the IEC SyC LVDC meeting in Toronto from 15 to 17 Sept. 2026 as Head of Delegation from India.
