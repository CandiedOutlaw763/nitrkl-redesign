About the Department

The Department of Mathematics, one of the oldest departments of NIT Rourkela (formerly known as REC Rourkela) is known for its quality. It offers Integrated MSc, MSc, and PhD programs in Mathematics. The department wants to concentrate on offering a thorough undergraduate and postgraduate curriculum, pertinent research, and professional prospects in India and overseas. The theoretical and computational parts of mathematics are maintained through the dual degree programs in the subject. The program is extremely well-liked by JEE/JAM aspirants.

Vision

To produce highly qualified and well-rounded mathematicians and researchers, technologists, and engineering professionals having skills in mathematics, who can provide leadership and service to the nation and the world.

Mission

To pursue creative research in mathematics and related disciplines to serve the needs of society, the scientific community, and industry, and also to develop a partnership with industry and government agencies.
