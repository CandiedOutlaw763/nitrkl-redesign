Biography

Dr. Sahendra Ram has been serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mining Engineering at the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Rourkela, since 2020. Prior to joining NIT Rourkela, he served at the CSIR–Central Institute of Mining and Fuel Research (CIMFR), Dhanbad, from 1999. He obtained his B.Tech. in Mining Engineering from Birsa Institute of Technology, Sindri, and completed his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad, India. He subsequently carried out postdoctoral research on “Numerical simulation for assessment of yielding characteristics of coal pillars at Great Depth (900m)” at Institute of Geonics of the Czech Academy of Sciences, Ostrava, Czech Republic Dr. Ram has over 26 years of research experience in underground coal mining, with a primary focus on continuous miner-based mass production technology. He has been involved in more than 125 in-house and industry-sponsored consultancy projects, including five science and technology (R&D) projects funded by the Ministry of Coal, Government of India the Coal India R&D Board TexMin, IIT (ISM), Dhanbad, as well as one international collaborative project. He has published over 85 papers in SCI- and Scopus-indexed journals and conference proceedings. He holds two patents related to the design of breaker line support and rib/snook systems for continuous miner-based mechanized depillaring. His research interests include underground coal mining methods, applied rock mechanics, numerical modelling, ground control, instrumentation, and strata monitoring. He has visited the USA, Australia, Poland, and the Czech Republic to deliver research talks at various international conferences.
