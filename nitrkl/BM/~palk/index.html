Biography

Dr. Kunal Pal is a Professor in the Department of Biotechnology and Medical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Rourkela. His work integrates biomaterials, AI-driven healthcare, and sustainable food systems. He holds a Ph.D. in Materials Science & Engineering from IIT-Kharagpur and has postdoctoral experience from Ryerson University, Canada. A highly recognized academic, Dr. Pal is consistently ranked among the Top 2% of scientists worldwide by Stanford/Elsevier, both for single-year and career-long impact. He serves as a national expert on committees for the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare and the Bureau of Indian Standards, Government of India. He has authored over 240 peer-reviewed publications, edited 11 books, and contributed to more than 70 book chapters. Dr. Pal’s pioneering research includes developing novel soft materials like oleogels and bigels for healthier fat alternatives and drug delivery, creating AI-enabled models for cardiac diagnosis from ECG signals, and designing low-cost IoT-based diagnostic devices for food analysis and healthcare. His innovative work has led to several granted patents for inventions like an IoT-enabled color analysis device and an amyloid-stabilized emulsion. As a research leader, he has secured funding as a Principal or Co-Investigator across 11 major projects from premier agencies like SERB, DST, and DBT. He also served as the Head of his department from 2021 to 2024, contributing significantly to its academic and institutional growth. His work has a strong societal impact, focusing on sustainable food technologies and affordable healthcare solutions.
