Biography

Dr. Mukesh Kumar Gupta is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Biology (UK), National Academy of Agricultural Sciences (NAAS), National Academy of Dairy Sciences (NADS), National Academy of Biological Sciences (NABS), National Academy of Environmental Sciences (NAES), Indian Association for Advancement of Veterinary Research (IAAVR), Academy of Environment and Life Sciences (AELS) and Society of Applied Biotechnology (SAB). He is an Executive Member of the Council of Asian Science Editors (CASE), HERITAGE Network (France), and Editorial Board Member of a few SCI-indexed Journals. He is a Board Member of the Animal Welfare Board of India (AWBI), Govt. of India, and Committee for Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals (CCSEA), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Govt. of India. He is known for his research on animal cloning, transgenics, in vitro spermatogenesis by tissue engineering, a quick method to detect DNA fragmentation, and solid surface vitrification. His work on modified solid surface vitrification is used worldwide for the cryopreservation of pig oocytes and embryos, algae, microalgae, and plant cells. He is a Subject Expert of Biotechnology and Biomedical Engineering for the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the National Testing Agency (NTA), and the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). He is currently on lien to the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) as Director of the National Animal Resource Facility for Biomedical Research (NARFBR), Hyderabad.
