Biography

Prof. Ramakrishna Biswal is Associate Professor in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences. His area of specialization is Psychology and Allied Sciences. He has successfully completed research projects in tribal development related issues. His research areas include but not limited to Developmental Psychology, Ethical Engineering and Sustainable Development. Apart from teaching and Research, Dr. Biswal is active in social services and volunteering activities like Blood donation, counselling people for their psychological issues, teaching the underprivileged children and conducting awareness sessions on Health, Education, Livelihood and Skill Development for the rural and urban populace.
