Biography

Dr. Harekrushna Sahoo currently working as a professor in the Department of Chemistry at NIT Rourkela. He joined NIT Rourkela in May 2012 as an Assistant Professor. Prior to that he pursued his PhD under the supervision of Prof. Werner M Nau in Germany (Jacobs University Bremen, 2006) followed by two postodctoral tenures (Prof. Lila M Gierasch at University of Massachusetts-Amherst, USA 2007 to 2009 and Prof. Petra Schwille at Technical University Dresden, Germany 2010-2011). Before joining NIT Rourkela, he was serving as a guest scientist in the group of Prof. Carsten Werner at Max-Bergmann Center, Dresden (Germany). His research group is working on protien biophysical chemistry (Stability, kinetics, dynamics, and aggregation), ionic liquids (synthesis and its biological applications) and nanobiotechnology (applications of nanochemistry in protein science).
